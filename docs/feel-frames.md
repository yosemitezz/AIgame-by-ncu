# Combat animation and impact presentation revision

## Visual contract

The hero is newly drawn from integer-grid joints, body, hood, cloak, hands and blade poses in `tools/generate_combat_frames.py`. No rendered models, translated frame copies, external character art or antialiasing were used. Cursor's committed source assets remain unchanged.

- Boss, enemies: native frame 64x64 or 80x80 as before. Hero: 144x128 frames, feet anchor (66,108), about 50px tall (long limbs, scarf, hood, curved blade), generated by `tools/generate_hero_atlas.py` from the joint rig in `tools/hero_rig.py` (do not re-run `tools/generate_combat_frames.py` for the hero, it would restore the old 64px art). Hero states include jump (rise/apex/fall), land, air_jump flip, crouch (in/out/hold/walk) and air_cut_* rows for airborne swings. The collider stays 14x26 and level metrics remain unchanged.
- Hero atlas: 70 cells, 8 columns. Four idle frames, six articulated run frames, four frames each for jump/fall/dash/parry/hurt/dead; three attacks each contain windup/attack/recover with four frames per stage.
- Each attack has separately authored shoulder, elbow, sword angle, hip, knees, feet and cloak. Cut 1 descends, cut 2 rises, cut 3 braces and thrusts with a front-foot commitment. Repeated settle poses across clips are intentional, not invented unique-frame counts.
- Colors: eleven visible RGB palette colors; transparency is real alpha. Nearest filtering and integer draw positions. No whole-character image rotation/scale animation.
- The shader-free hit flash is restricted to the struck actor silhouette; no full-screen flash.

## Runtime adapter

`presentation/actor_renderer.gd` exposes static `paint(canvas,kind,state,frame,facing)` for the existing integration painter. Hero states `windup`, `attack`, `recover` select the actual combo 1..3 and phase frame 0..3. Other enemy/Boss kinds retain their original atlas and a brief silhouette hit flash, without fractional scale.

Additional helpers: `hero_region(state,frame,combo)`, `weapon_tip_local(combo,frame)`, `silhouette_texture()`. Blade-tip positions are generated from the same pose data as the actual drawn sword, not an unrelated visual arc.

`presentation/combat_vfx.gd` is a presentation-only Node2D. `configure(game)` attaches `warning_issued`; root forwards `emit_fx` to `emit_effect(kind,at,direction,power)`. `clear_hostile()` clears warning tells, enemy arcs, all stale blade arcs/trails; ambient pickups and friendly dash silhouettes are not treated as hostile. It never changes health, animation stage, Engine.time_scale or tree pause.

`slash` power 1/2/3 selects the hero combo. Its sprite frame is bound to the real player owner, attack_serial and normalized ACTIVE-phase progress. Local hit-stop freezes the crescent with the body/blade; recovery, cancellation, owner destruction or a new attack removes that crescent immediately. The already-sampled tip trail alone fades for 0.16s. Enemy and Boss swings must use `enemy_slash`. Threat tells are amber outlines, projectiles remain owned by gameplay. Hit feedback uses a 5-frame core/stroke strip plus 4–7 short debris chips. Parry uses a hollow diamond and hard cross, leaving the playfield visible. Dash snapshots the actual hero pose and retains at most three low-alpha silhouettes for 0.15 seconds.

Budgets: 64 effect entries, 72 debris chips, 3 dash ghosts, 12 warnings, 8 points per blade trail. These are caps, not target particle counts.

## Actual open-source adaptation

The crescent geometry and start/update/end blade-trail lifecycle were adapted from `CodingQuests/godot-hitbox-hurtbox`, pinned commit `7ccc10925a07960772d83db970d56fef875a983d`, `scripts/WeaponController.gd`. MIT copyright 2026 CodingQuests; retained license: `third_party/codingquests/LICENSE`.

`make_arc_points` in the pixel generator preserves the source's outer radius `offset + size.x * 0.5`, inner radius `outer * 0.45`, clamped span up to 130 degrees and 10-step outer/inner polygon. It is rounded and rasterized at five authored sweep angles rather than drawn as a smooth scaled polygon. The third combo uses an original thrust stroke for a distinct shape.

`_start_trail`, `_update_trail`, `_end_trail` in CombatVFX follow real ACTIVE-phase serial changes, sample actual sword-tip coordinates, cap at eight points (source cap twelve) and fade only after active ends over 0.16 seconds. No upstream demo project or code was executed.

## Evidence and limits

Local ignored evidence is generated to `artifacts/feel-*`: combo/motion/VFX contact sheets, native-pose before/after comparison, asset hashes/palette/unique-frame report, and Godot parse log. These are fixed pose inspections rather than gameplay. Parent integration verifies runtime capture, local hit-stop and timing logic. Actual responsiveness and difficulty remain for the user's own play test.

Slash and hit sheets (slash_1..3.png, hit.png) are angular: faceted blade crescents, a crystal lance for the finisher and a hexagram impact, written by 	ools/generate_angular_fx.py. Run it after 	ools/generate_combat_frames.py, which writes the older rounded versions. Live effects in fx/fx_layer.gd use hexagon rings plus hexagram and crystal shapes.

Hit punch (combat/impact_feedback.gd): hit stop 0.058 s light / 0.115 s heavy, camera kick up to 11 px, shake trauma 0.4 to 1.0 and a camera zoom punch of up to 7.5 percent that eases back (sample_zoom). F2 (reduced motion) disables the kick, shake and zoom. Ring shapes are round by default; sides: 4 gives a square diamond and sides: 6 a hexagon. Arcs take acets (22 smooth, 5 or 6 cut crystal).

Strike reach (Player.HIT_REACH): 62 / 70 / 80 px per combo stage plus the enemy body radius, with a vertical tolerance of 40 px (HERO_HIT_HALF_HEIGHT in main.gd). This matches the swing effects, which draw out to roughly 70 to 90 px. Impact sounds (udio/impact_sfx.gd) are layered 32 kHz synths: body thump, noise crack, metal ring and a glassy shatter tail on heavy hits, plus stun, launch, air jump and hurt sounds.

HUD (ui/hud.gd) is Undertale-style: black panels with hard white frames, a red soul heart, a yellow-on-red HP bar that grows with max HP, four orange command buttons (attack, parry, dash, map), a typewriter dialogue box for zone hints and narration, a boss bar with soul-heart guard pips, and a game-over screen where the soul cracks and bursts. It only reads the hud_updated state dictionary.

Dark fantasy look: the level palette is bruised violet and ash stone with ember gold and a blood moon (levels/bellkeep.gd), with stained-glass lancet windows, gothic spires, dead trees, tombstones, gargoyles on the gates, hanging cages and bone and blood details on the ground. fx/dark_atmosphere.gd adds an ambient tint, additive warm and cold light pools at every torch, candle and the shrine, ground fog, falling ash, rising embers and a vignette. It only draws and reads the camera. The TAB map shows zone bands, room outlines and a fog of war that lifts as the hero advances.

Shadow follower and hit feel: the hero's afterimages are near-black silhouettes with a thin violet rim (presentation/ghost_shadow.gdshader), the coloured scarf is gone and the body ribbon is dark smoke. fx/hero_smear.gd stretches five pooled copies of the real sprite behind fast movement and pulses on every landed hit, and the speed lines in fx/motion_blur.gd are denser and brighter (F3 strong mode doubles them). The old rainbow and flow ramps are now furnace ember and icy blade light. A landed hit stops the lunge and pushes the hero back 85 to 140 px/s for 0.1 s (HIT_RECOIL_SPEED in ctors/player.gd), starting after hit stop and cancelled by dash or parry.

## Knight and three swords

The hero is now a dark-fantasy knight (helmet with visor slit, steel plate, crimson tabard and cape). The rig in `tools/hero_rig.py` draws three sword sets into separate sheets with identical grids, states and anchor: light (`hero_atlas.png`), heavy (`hero_atlas_heavy.png`) and great (`hero_atlas_great.png`), plus matching flash, silhouette and parry sheets. `manifest.json` carries `hero.weapons` with per-sword atlas names and weapon tips.

Swords: light is the original arming sword (damage 1/1/2, fast). Heavy is a broad falchion (damage 2/2/3, slower, short and wide arc, more hit stop and recoil, ember FX). Great is a two-handed greatsword (damage 2/3/4, reach about 236 to 280 px, slow windup, biggest hit stop, recoil and camera kick, gold FX and a ground ring). Numbers live in `data/hero_attack_profiles.json` under `weapons`; the light table is the unchanged base. Switch with Q (next), K (previous) or 1, 2, 3. A swap waits until the current swing, dash or guard recoil ends, and is refused in story-safe sections. The choice survives reloads through a tree meta key.
